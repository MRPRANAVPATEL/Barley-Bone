      (01) The house

      We make the everyday things for dogs the way we’d want them for ourselves: quietly well made, honest about materials, and better after every muddy walk.

      Barley & Bone began with a single leash, stitched by hand at a kitchen table, because nothing in the shops was worth keeping.

      Today a small network of workshops makes the collection in short runs — leather from a tannery that still uses oak bark, linen washed until it forgets it was new. When a piece sells out, it stays sold out until the next run.

      (04) Principles

      What we believe.

      Four rules we hold ourselves to — and will happily be held to.

      01

      Made to be dirty.

      Everything we make is meant to be washed, chewed, rained on and worn soft. If it looks better after a year, we did it right.

      02

      Fewer, better things.

      A handful of pieces each season, in short runs. No flash sales, no filler, nothing you’ll be sorry you bought.

      03

      Honest materials.

      Vegetable-tanned leather, organic cotton, natural rubber, stoneware. We name every material and tell you where it comes from.

      04

      Dogs first.

      Every piece is tested by a rotating pack of real dogs before it ships — and retired if they don’t approve.
